GeoNotary

An app that certifies the user's location with legal validity: timestamped records, a cryptographic digest and an identity model based on movement patterns. It is used in legal defence and to prove tax residency.

Image2Biomass

Project 03 · open source · Kaggle

Pasture biomass from a single photo

CSIRO – Image2Biomass competition on Kaggle: estimate the biomass of a pasture plot from a single top-down image, telling live matter from dead, with only 357 training photos. We compared four computer-vision strategies and wrote the report as a scientific paper.

  • Embeddings from a vision foundation model with regression heads, knowledge distillation with privileged information, a ConvNeXt and EfficientNet ensemble, and classical descriptors with Random Forest.
  • Detection and correction of data leakage in validation, so the reported results reflect real performance.
  • Team project for the competition, designed to put our Deep Learning knowledge and skills to the test on a real problem.
PythonPyTorchscikit-learnKaggle

05 · About me

From code to
product in production

I joined GeoNotary in December 2025 and from day one my work went all the way to production: the app I changed in the morning was in customers' hands by the afternoon. In a three-person team there are no departments, so I've worked across mobile, backend, servers and security.

The most valuable lesson so far has been to measure before forming an opinion. A product had been failing for months and everyone assumed the algorithm was to blame. Analysing the real data showed that nine out of ten failures were something else. Seeing it work after weeks of analysis is what I've enjoyed most about this job.

I work for a company whose software ends up in front of a judge, and that has made me careful before claiming something works. I'm looking for a team where I can keep learning, with people who review my code and debate decisions.
